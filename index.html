🌈 About Us

One of Ahmedabad's oldest & most reputed preschools

Fairyland Pre-School is a well-established kindergarten bringing the Montessori Method of education to children in Maninagar since 1999. We recognize the vital importance of the first five years in a child's development.

The child's powers of absorption are at their peak during these sensitive years — when lifelong attitudes and learning patterns are established. Our specially screened teachers are selected for their passion for imparting more than just knowledge to children.

Fairyland comes alive with the zest and creativity of children, where they find company, friendship, and the perfect surroundings for learning, knowledge, and education.

🎓 Montessori Method of Education

We follow a holistic approach to cultivating social skills and life skills, helping children build compassion, confidence, creativity, and self-reliance from the very beginning.

📚 Our Programs

Five programs, one magical journey

From toddler to school-ready — we nurture every stage of your little one's journey with expert Montessori-based care and joyful learning.

🍼

Toddler Care

Ages 1.5 – 2.5 yrs

Gentle first steps into school life — sensory play, emotional security, and social bonding in a nurturing space.

🌸

Nursery

Ages 2.5 – 3.5 yrs

Foundational skills through storytelling, music, art, and play — awakening curiosity and a love for learning.

⭐

Jr. K.G.

Ages 3.5 – 4.5 yrs

Structured activities for language, early numeracy, creativity, and building peer relationships confidently.

🚀

Sr. K.G.

Ages 4.5 – 6 yrs

School-readiness program: reading, writing, logical thinking, and social skills for a confident big-school start.

🌞

Day Care Center

Full-day Care

Safe, supervised, joyful full-day care blending structured activities with free play — a lifesaver for busy families.
